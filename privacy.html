Privacy Policy

Nikaro: Smart Flashcards • Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Privacy-First Architecture

Welcome to Nikaro ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details our data practices and principles, particularly that your study material belongs entirely to you.

1. User Content & Local Storage

Nikaro is designed offline-first:

  • All flashcard decks, notes, study history, repetition intervals, and user tags are stored locally in an encrypted/private SQLite database directly on your device.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest the contents of your flashcards or learning materials.

2. Diagnostics, Crash Reports & Telemetry

To ensure high performance, reliable spaced repetition scheduling, and rapid resolution of software defects, the app utilizes industry-standard crash reporting:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: In the event of an unexpected crash or freeze, Crashlytics transmits non-personally identifiable diagnostic crash traces, operating system versions, and device model identifiers. This data is used strictly for debugging and stability improvements.
  • Google Play Services: Manages standard application installation, version validation, and anonymized performance metrics as governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Nikaro requests only the permissions necessary to provide its core study functions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / Alarms): Used exclusively to deliver scheduled review reminders and study notifications configured by you.
  • Audio / Microphone (Optional): Used only when you choose to record custom pronunciation audio for your flashcards. Recorded audio is stored locally on your device.
  • Photo Library / Storage Access (Optional): Used if you attach images to your flashcard notes.

4. Optional Cloud Synchronization & User Accounts

Using Nikaro with an account is completely optional. You can use the app 100% offline without ever creating an account. If you choose to enable cloud backup and multi-device synchronization:

  • Authentication is securely processed via Supabase. Your credentials and session tokens are encrypted and stored securely using OS-level security (iOS Keychain and Android Keystore).
  • Synchronized decks, cards, and review states are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

In full compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, you retain full ownership of your data and can permanently delete it at any time:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the app at any time by going to Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account credentials, cloud decks, notes, and study logs from our servers.
  • Offline Data: Because local study materials are stored on your device, uninstalling the application or clearing app data immediately removes all local flashcard content.
  • Deletion Request by Email: You may also request deletion of your account and any associated cloud data by emailing us at nikaro.app@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or flashcard content to third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms. There are zero targeted third-party ad SDKs embedded in the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Nikaro does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate measures.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect product enhancements or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published directly on this website with an updated revision date.
